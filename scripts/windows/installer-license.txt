Lausu - Terms of Service and Consent

By installing this software you agree to the following terms.

1. LOCAL PROCESSING BY DEFAULT
Lausu processes your voice, transcribed text, and metadata locally
on your device by default. The app itself sends nothing anywhere
except for the network uses listed in sections 2 and 5 below, and
only a cloud feature you explicitly enable ever sends your audio
or text to a third party.

2. FEATURE CONSENT
Certain features contact third parties, and only when you enable them:
- Cloud speech recognition providers: when you configure a cloud
  speech recognition provider and it is the active backend, audio
  recordings are sent to that provider for transcription, under that
  provider's terms and privacy policy (OpenAI, Groq, or Deepgram,
  depending on the provider you select).
- LLM text polishing: sends transcribed text (not audio) to your
  configured LLM API for grammar and punctuation cleanup when you
  have granted consent.
- Model downloads: local speech models are downloaded from Hugging
  Face, which reveals your IP address to a US-based third party
  (Hugging Face, Inc.). Audio is never sent for downloads. Downloads
  occur only when you explicitly initiate them and have granted consent.
- Media links: transcribing a web link contacts that linked site
  directly from your device to fetch its audio stream.
- Offline engine pack: the offline transcription engine (about
  180 MB) is downloaded automatically in the background from the
  official release server when needed. No separate user consent is
  required; the download is silent and only runs when the app needs
  the pack. It reveals only your IP address and app version.

3. VOICE BIOMETRIC ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
Local voice recordings may be considered biometric data under the
Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) and GDPR
Article 9. You grant consent for local voice-biometric processing
when you enable recording. Purpose: transcribing your speech into
text on your device. Retention: raw audio is not kept after
transcription - only the transcribed text is kept, in your local
history database (kept up to 90 days by default, configurable;
favorites are exempt until deleted). Microphone test clips are
deleted automatically after 5 minutes. You may delete history,
recovery entries, and test clips at any time from within the app.

4. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY FOR RECORDINGS
You are solely responsible for how you use recording. Record only
yourself, or others who have agreed to be recorded. You must comply
with the recording, wiretapping, and two-party/all-party consent
laws of your jurisdiction, and with the terms and privacy policies
of any third-party provider whose API key you configure (cloud
speech providers, LLM providers). The authors operate no server
that receives your audio, text, or API keys.

5. SOFTWARE UPDATES
The application periodically checks for updates against the official
release server. This reveals only your IP address and app version.
No audio, text, or API keys are included in update checks.

6. REVOCATION
You may revoke any cloud, LLM, or model-download consent at any
time in Settings > Privacy with the same ease as granting it.
Revoked features refuse to run until consent is re-granted. The
offline engine pack does not use a user toggle: it downloads
automatically when required (see section 2).
