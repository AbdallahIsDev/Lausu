Privacy disclosure · Lausu v1.0.0

Audio and data stay on your machine

Lausu is an offline-first desktop application. The app sends zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero crash reports.

  • Local audio processing

    When using local speech recognition (Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, or Parakeet Ultra), Lausu processes microphone audio entirely on your device. Audio leaves your computer only when you explicitly turn on a cloud provider or ask Media transcription to fetch a public web URL.

  • Voice biometrics and raw audio

    Voice recordings can qualify as biometric data under regional laws such as GDPR Article 9 and Illinois BIPA. Lausu records audio solely to transcribe speech on your device and discards raw microphone buffers after transcription finishes; only text is written to your local history database.

  • Model weights from Hugging Face

    Downloading local speech or TTS weights connects directly to Hugging Face the first time you fetch a model. Hugging Face receives your IP address as part of that HTTPS download. Once saved locally, inference runs without network access.

  • Opt-in cloud providers

    If you enter an API key for OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, Google Gemini, or Anthropic, Lausu stores the key in your operating system keyring rather than config.json. Cloud recognition remains off until you grant explicit per-domain transmission consent, which you can revoke at any time in Settings.

  • Local SQLite storage

    Configuration, custom vocabulary, text snippets, and dictation history live in your local user profile directory. History retains entries for 90 days by default and can be searched, exported to TXT, CSV, or JSON, or cleared whenever you choose.
